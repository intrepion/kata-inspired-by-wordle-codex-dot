# Morrowmark language

**Dispatch**: The shared five-letter puzzle for one UTC calendar day.

**Fieldnote**: One submitted five-letter guess and its position clues.

**Exact (✓)**: A letter in its correct position.

**Elsewhere (↔)**: A letter present in the answer in a different position.

**Absent (×)**: No unmatched copy of this letter remains in the answer.

**Practice**: A separate, replayable puzzle that never contributes to daily records.

**Streak**: Consecutive UTC dispatch dates won, ending today or yesterday.
