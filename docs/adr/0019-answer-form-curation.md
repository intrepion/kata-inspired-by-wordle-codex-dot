# Answer-only plural/past filtering with explicit ambiguity policy

The user requested removing plural noun and past-tense verb forms from curated answers while retaining them as accepted guesses. This is a separate change after the published commit. No publication authority was inferred from that earlier approval; the user subsequently explicitly authorized committing/pushing this filtering update, without deployment. Start from the pinned SCOWLcommon35 set(3,493), keep its existing11,193 accepted guesses byte-identical, and use AGID2016.01.19 at official revision5ef55f9c42730ebe4394a78b77855468a6f15dd2. Raw source, README, filtered rows and complete permission notices have pinned hashes; upstream permits database/output redistribution with retained contributor notices. No copied source code, dictionary API dependency or runtime morphology work.

Parse AGID’s noun plural slots and verb finite past slots, including its be special case and ordinary combined past/participle slots. Do not treat standalone participle-only slots, present/third-person forms, comparative/superlative slots or suffix patterns as exclusions. Reject uncertain POS, marked alternative forms and variants≥2 as automatic evidence. Never truncate apostrophes into a different word. Identity forms(SHEEP, BURST, SPLIT) retain their base sense.

Default: exclude a qualified targeted form even if AGID invents a base record. Reviewed exceptions keep a clear common independent sense—not a rare alternate, merely derived adjective or technical-only homograph. Examples: FOUND(establish), WOUND(injury), SPOKE(wheel part), CLOVE(spice), MOPED(vehicle), GENUS(singular classification), MEANS(singular method). MEDIA, TIRED, BURNT, SHOOK/CHOSE remain excluded; their collective/derived/rare readings do not qualify. HIDED/SADES are reviewed exclusions for uncertain source mappings. Reasons are explicit in answer-form-overrides.json. Other verb forms including present-only SEEMS/LOSES and participle-only EATEN/TAKEN remain intentionally. This is a reproducible curation policy with source gaps, not perfect classification of all senses.
