Twenty batches cover the exact sorted missing-word set. New text is AI-authored and independently model-reviewed, not human/native-certified or dictionary-provided translations. Translation, Spanish context and English rendering must agree. Examples should be natural neutral Mexican Spanish, with exact accented whole-word targets, varied real contexts, and no metalinguistic filler. Do not force an obscure first source sense when a clear ordinary meaning is more useful.

Independent reviewers inspect other-author batches, correct meaning/grammar/regional/translation issues and sign final file hashes. The generator refuses incomplete, self-reviewed, unapproved or stale-hash data. Structural validation catches missing/blank/duplicate records, placeholders, invented metadata, non-NFC text, missing targets, duplicate Spanish examples and obviously untranslated sentences. These checks cannot prove semantic accuracy; independent linguistic review is essential and can still miss errors. Notes and the review manifest retain the provenance and limits.
