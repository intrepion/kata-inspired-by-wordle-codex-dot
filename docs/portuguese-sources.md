# Portuguese regional sources and selection

Português → Brasil and Português → Portugal use separate, conservative 3–11-letter lists. They share many ordinary words but never fall back to each other's dictionary. This is selected coverage, not every Portuguese word or inflection, and not native-human certification. English meanings stay local.

The first reviewed release contains **1,453 Brazil answers / 7,227 accepted source spellings** (7,206 folded keys) and **2,007 Portugal answers / 9,536 source spellings** (9,498 folded keys). Every accepted spelling has a bundled source meaning. At least 61 answers are available at every length in each variety.

## Pinned source evidence

`src/data/portuguese/source.json` records the exact snapshot URLs, SHA256 hashes, byte counts, licenses and transformations. Files under `public/third-party/pt-BR/` and `pt-PT/` preserve the original regional dictionary sources byte-for-byte. Their README encodings are preserved, including the upstream Latin-1 Portugal notice. The `.aff` declarations for both dictionaries are checked as UTF-8.

- **Brazil:** VERO 3.2, Raimundo Santos Moura and contributors, from LibreOffice dictionaries commit `c0865a85eb67dc377f2cf8e8fe2c4fa9e53f0dcb`. Its README explicitly offers **LGPL version 3** and a Mozilla Public License without a version. We choose **LGPL-3.0**, not an inferred MPL version. The original `.dic`, `.aff`, README, LGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 texts are bundled.
- **Portugal:** official [Natura hunspell-pt_PT-20251001](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/download/sources/Dictionaries/hunspell/hunspell-pt_PT-20251001.tar.gz), the post-AO1990 release. The separate `preao` archive is not used. The source [AO feed](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/download/sources/Dictionaries/feed-ao.xml) and release index distinguish these tracks. Its README/COPYING explicitly offers GPL-2.0, LGPL-2.1 or **MPL-1.1**; we choose MPL-1.1. Authors are José João de Almeida, Rui Vilela and Alberto Simões, Universidade do Minho.
- **Lexical evidence and English definitions:** English Wiktionary Portuguese entries, provided by the [Kaikki postprocessed per-language snapshot](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Portuguese/), **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Only lowercase headwords, POS, senses, labels, form/alternative references and input-line hashes are retained. No quotations, examples, media or etymologies are imported. The snapshot does not supply exact article revision IDs. Runtime entries retain original headwords and article links.
- **Frequency:** wordfreq 3.0.2 at revision `372f6dbb3bd2ad4b675a3b18b94d44d0dd5fea8b`, **CC BY-SA 4.0 data**. Its software MIT license does not relicense the data. Mixed-region historical spelling frequency is neither current regional frequency nor sense-specific evidence.

## Regional membership and answer policy

The importer selects Wiktionary headwords with 3–11 NFC letters and spelling Zipf frequency at least 2.5. It does not invent words by stripping punctuation. The generator checks those lexical spellings against exact lowercase headword rows in the region's pinned dictionary, stripping dictionary flags/morphological metadata rather than affixes. Forbidden entries are excluded. Full Hunspell affix expansion is deliberately not performed, so common inflections and some ordinary words may be absent.

One bounded exception is explicit: **Brazil CASA** is generated by VERO's exact `casar/ajkLMYÀÂ` row. The generator verifies this single-row expansion with the existing pinned nspell dependency. CASA's **noun meaning** comes independently from its Wiktionary noun entry, not from the source verb conjugation. This exception avoids rejecting a basic word without claiming exhaustive morphology coverage.

Answer candidates need Zipf at least 4.3 or a small explicit regional/common-word exception, plus a qualifying standalone sense. The generator rejects marked rare, archaic, offensive, alternative-only and form-only senses and labels for the opposite region. Candidate selection is not the final answer decision: `review-br.json` and `review-pt.json` explicitly classify every candidate. Independent model review may hold forms, misleading homographs, uncommon senses, regional mismatches and unresolved spelling references. A source-backed regional spellchecker entry alone does not make a word a good answer. This review is not native-human certification.

Guesses are broader than answers and may have uncommon, historical, inflected or opposite-region senses retained with labels. Being present in a regional spellchecker is not a claim that every sense is used in that region. Lists intentionally overlap. A word may be accepted without being an answer.

## Accents, collisions and meanings

Canonical answers preserve accents and ç. Gameplay comparison folds marks to plain uppercase A–Z. For example, AVÓ and AVÔ share AVO. Both can be accepted/defined, but at most one canonical answer is selected for a folded key. `collisions-br.json` and `collisions-pt.json` enumerate all lexical collisions; these are not silently discarded.

Definitions are grouped by folded key while retaining each exact `sourceWord`. An accented reveal can select its exact spelling's senses; plain AVO can expose the alternative spellings with separate source links. No gloss is manufactured merely to avoid a gap. Form/alternative references remain literal references, not automatically resolved translations. The generator checks at least five distinct answers and one distinct folded-seam Smashare pair for every length; application tests cover all five modes and saves.

## Reproduce

1. Download the exact lexical and frequency artifacts identified by `source.json` to a local staging directory. Their full snapshot hashes are checked before extraction.
2. Run `python3 scripts/import-portuguese.py PORTUGUESE.jsonl SMALL_PT.msgpack.gz --check` to verify the retained evidence and manifest.
3. Run `node scripts/generate-portuguese.mjs --verify-regions --check` for byte-identical regional membership, review completeness, answer selection, definitions, collision audit and coverage. This uses only committed local sources and the existing nspell dependency for CASA.
4. To intentionally change source versions, update the pinned importer and review the changed candidates with `node scripts/generate-portuguese.mjs --verify-regions --candidates`. Do not carry an old review forward without checking the new evidence. Generate outputs without `--check` only after review.
5. Run `npm run check` and the Portuguese browser/mode tests before release. A successful data regeneration alone does not verify the app.

`coverage.json` records per-length answer, original guess, folded guess, defined-guess and seam-pair counts, review hashes, membership hashes and output hashes. Dictionaries and definitions are generated independently of saved user games. Neither a new list nor this documentation authorizes publication.

## Rights and modifications

The project's MIT code license does not replace data rights. Wiktionary/wordfreq lexical evidence, selected lexical spellings, English senses and modifications retain CC BY-SA 4.0. VERO and Natura sources and their regional membership evidence retain LGPL-3.0 / MPL-1.1 rights respectively. Validation does not blanket-relicense either source or the application code.

The bundled original Hunspell sources are unmodified. The source and transformations for derived regional membership are supplied in the generator, membership files, manifests and reviews. Full notices are bundled at `third-party/pt/NOTICE.txt`; upstream `.dic`/`.aff` files remain available beside their licenses. These files can be modified/replaced and redistributed under their applicable licenses; no additional restriction is imposed. No live source is contacted during play.
